# Google Play compliance baseline (2026-10)

- Target Android 16 / API 36 for new Play submissions after 2026-08-31.
- Digital goods (gem, lives, boosters, digital gifts, subscriptions/passes) use Google Play Billing where required.
- No cash-out and no general peer-to-peer money transfer.
- Contacts: default to system Contact Picker / Sharesheet; do not bulk-upload the address book. Store only explicitly selected invite data, and hash phone numbers when only matching is needed.
- UGC/chat: Terms, report, block, moderation, minor safeguards and enforcement workflow are product requirements, not later add-ons.
- Random paid rewards/loot boxes are avoided in v1; if introduced later, odds must be clearly disclosed before purchase.
